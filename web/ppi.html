Oracle

Pull model: prices are signed off-chain for free. A consumer submits them in its own transaction and pays that gas. Feed: /api/v1/price-signed

What this is not. Not a claim on physical commodities, not a total-return or futures-roll index, and not audited by a third party. Minting is disabled: there is no deposit token, so no one can put funds into the protocol. The index exists to be read and, eventually, licensed.

For venues

Listing or licensing PPI: the methodology is public, the contracts are verified, and the oracle feed is free to read. Start at operators or read the rulebook.
