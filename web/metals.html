Two gold tokens, not one, on purpose. PAXG is issued by Paxos (a US national trust bank) and is unavailable to EU residents under MiCA; XAUT is issued in the BVI with Swiss vaults. Holding both spreads issuer and jurisdiction risk — that is the only reason a basket exists. If you just want gold, one token is simpler and cheaper. Silver is KAG: one token, 1:1 claim on Kinesis silver, issued by KMS Labs S.A. (Panama).

Balances are read from Ethereum mainnet. Only canonical issuer deployments are counted; bridged copies are excluded because they add bridge risk on top of issuer risk. Ounces are exact (1 PAXG = 1 XAUT = 1 KAG = 1 fine troy ounce); the USD figure is indicative and is blanked rather than guessed when no price is available.
