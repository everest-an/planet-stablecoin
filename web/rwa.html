The collateral layer for tokenized commodities

Issuers bring real assets. Custodians hold them. Planet does the onboarding, the portfolio, the valuation and the collateral risk. Lenders supply USDC; borrowers pledge tokenized commodity assets.

Roles

roleresponsible for
Issuerthe real asset and the legal claim
Custodianthe underlying asset
Planetasset onboarding, basket, valuation, collateral risk, matching
LenderUSDC / USDT liquidity
Borrowertokenized commodity collateral, borrowing demand

What makes it different

  • Data-quality pricing. The same collateral carries a different loan-to-value when its price data degrades — caution lowers LTV, frozen blocks new borrowing. The PPI risk states power this.
  • Multi-issuer aggregation. One basket can hold assets from several issuers, instead of a single issuer's own market.
  • Commodities first. Tokenized gold and commodity ETF shares are the first collateral class, not an afterthought.

Scope of the first MVP

One collateral class (tokenized gold), one borrow asset (USDC), over-collateralised, whitelisted participants, liquidation through external venues. It is a lending product, not a stablecoin and not an exchange.

Status

Specification stage. The previous products — the PPI perpetual and the PLT token — are discontinued and delisted; nothing was deposited into the discontinued pool. The PPI index stays live as the valuation and risk layer. Not externally audited; no deposit product is open.
