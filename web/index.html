[ research ]what Planet is building on top: a commodity purchasing-power index¬
  • An on-chain commodity purchasing-power index expressed as the PLT token.
  • A synthetic index token (ERC-20) — PLT on Arbitrum One (index-only), and on Arbitrum Sepolia with mint / burn / redemption accounting.
  • Prices sourced by an off-chain relayer from up to four independent channels (Yahoo primary + confirmations), pushed by an authorised relayer; the SignedPriceOracle M-of-N path is live on Arbitrum One (2-of-3).
  • Governed by an owner + timelock (Propose → delay → Execute), multisig-ready.
  • Not a fully-collateralised stablecoin. Mints are collateralised by test USDC, but there is no real commodity reserve.
  • Not pegged 1:1 to a basket — PLT is an index, its market price may diverge.
  • Not externally audited. Self-audited with reproducible test suites; the perpetual market holds real USDC and should be treated as beta software.
  • Not a DEX. Planet builds no AMM; trading happens on external venues.
[ 03 ]how it works¬
  1. 01

    Basket

    Owner configures a weighted basket (e.g. WTI 40% · Gold 35% · Copper 25%) and locks base prices on-chain.

  2. 02

    Multi-source oracle

    A relayer reads up to four channels: Yahoo is the primary, Sina/Tencent/Eastmoney are confirmations within 0.5% (not a median). With no confirmation the reading is marked caution.

  3. 03

    On-chain PPI

    PlanetCore checks the timestamp, weights and jump guard, then recomputes the PPI from the live basket. The signed M-of-N path is available through SignedPriceOracle; the live path is an authorised relayer.

  4. 04

    Mint / burn

    Deposit to mint PLT at the current PPI (fee split 30/50/20, +10% risk fund). Burns are executed by the core against the PLT token, so token supply and core accounting stay in step.

[ 06 ]how to use¬
  1. 01

    Get a wallet

    Reading needs no wallet. To transact, install an EVM wallet (e.g. MetaMask or Rabby) and keep your recovery phrase safe.

  2. 02

    Get testnet ETH

    Mint/redeem runs on Arbitrum Sepolia — get free test ETH from a faucet (e.g. faucet.arbitrum.io). Reading the index needs no wallet and no gas.

  3. 03

    Open the app

    Open the — it renders straight from the contract and needs no wallet. Planet never sees your keys.

  4. 04

    Mint / burn

    Open to mint or redeem against the Arbitrum contracts. Minting pulls test USDC from your wallet (transferFrom) at the on-chain index; redeeming pays it back out of the reserve.

[ 07 ]trust & safety¬
  • Non-custodial — the app never holds keys; you sign in your own wallet, so the site cannot move funds.
  • Verifiable on-chain — every price update is written on-chain by the authorised relayer and the index is computed on-chain, so the number on screen is recomputable from the contract.
  • Fail-safe oracle — four independent price channels (Yahoo, Sina, Tencent, Eastmoney) with a confirmation rule, staleness and price-jump guards, and a caution flag when a reading cannot be confirmed.
  • Governance — owner actions can be migrated to a multisig and gated by a timelock (Propose → delay → Execute).
  • Honest limits — not audited yet; on Arbitrum One the relayer path is disabled and the signed oracle is 2-of-3 with threshold 2; Phase 1 holds no real reserve (see “what it is not”).
