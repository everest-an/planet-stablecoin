Planet (PLT) aggregates the real prices of energy, metals and agriculture into one on-chain index — a synthetic index token (TEP-74), verifiable on TON and mintable from your wallet.

[ 01 ]what it is¬
  • An on-chain commodity purchasing-power index expressed as the PLT token.
  • A synthetic index token (TEP-74) on TON, with mint / burn / redemption accounting.
  • Prices sourced by an off-chain relayer from two independent feeds, verified on-chain by ed25519 signatures.
  • Governed by an owner + timelock (Propose → delay → Execute), multisig-ready.
[ 02 ]what it is not¬
  • Not a fully-collateralised stablecoin. Phase 1 holds no real commodity reserve.
  • Not pegged 1:1 to a basket — PLT is an index, its market price may diverge.
  • Not audited yet. Testnet only; do not use with real value.
  • Not a DEX. Planet builds no AMM; trading happens on external venues.
[ 03 ]how it works¬
  1. 01

    Basket

    Owner configures a weighted basket (e.g. WTI 40% · Gold 35% · Copper 25%) and locks base prices on-chain.

  2. 02

    Multi-source oracle

    A relayer reads two independent feeds, takes the median, and signs the payload. Divergence beyond 2% freezes the asset out of the index.

  3. 03

    On-chain PPI

    PlanetCore verifies the signature (M-of-N signer set), guards against stale / jumped prices, and recomputes the PPI from the live basket.

  4. 04

    Mint / burn

    Deposit to mint PLT at the current PPI (fee split 30/50/20, +10% risk fund). Burns mirror through the jetton minter back to core accounting.

[ 06 ]how to use¬
  1. 01

    Get a wallet

    Install a TON wallet (e.g. Tonkeeper) and keep your recovery phrase safe.

  2. 02

    Get testnet TON

    This is TON testnet — request free test TON from a faucet (e.g. the Telegram bot @testgiver_ton_bot).

  3. 03

    Open the app

    Open the Mini App and tap Connect wallet. Planet never sees your keys.

  4. 04

    Mint / burn

    Enter an amount and approve in your wallet; burn PLT back through the jetton wallet.

[ 07 ]trust & safety¬
  • Non-custodial — the app never holds keys; you sign in your own wallet, so the site cannot move funds.
  • Verifiable on-chain — every price update is ed25519-signed and verified on TON; the index is computed on-chain.
  • Fail-safe oracle — two independent feeds, median, M-of-N signers, staleness + price-jump guards, auto-freeze on divergence.
  • Governance — owner actions can be migrated to a multisig and gated by a timelock (Propose → delay → Execute).
  • Honest limits — not audited yet; Phase 1 holds no real reserve (see “what it is not”).
