The window

Dubai has already built the rails. Nobody has filled them.

In October 2025 DMCC and VARA signed a framework specifically to tokenise commodities, with pilots in gold and diamonds. The first instrument — a 1,971 kg silver bar — was issued under it in September 2026. DMCC counts 1,500+ precious-metals firms, 700+ web3 companies and 26,000 members.

What exists

  • The regulator's framework (VARA — Category 2 issuance needs no issuer licence, only a licensed distributor)
  • The registry (DMCC Tradeflow — already handles gold-backed digital assets)
  • The demand: African & Asian traders who cannot get bank finance

What is missing

  • A production-grade issuance stack others can adopt
  • A verified oracle that survives a bad feed
  • Governance that cannot touch custody
  • Documentation good enough to speed up an audit

How it works

Failure is isolated. Governance is bound. Custody is never ours.

1  You define one instrument  (one commodity · one jurisdiction · one client group)
2  We deploy a branded instance on TESTNET against your asset and your data   ← zero risk, zero cost
3  You take it to compliance and to your auditor        (we supply the audit pack)
4  Optional: mainnet, placed by your licensed distributor
5  We operate the stack. You own the product and the client relationship.

Engineering that matters

  • Two independent price feeds, median, ed25519-signed, verified on-chain against an M-of-N signer set with distinct-signer enforcement
  • Guards: strictly increasing timestamps, a single-update price-jump limit, automatic freeze on source divergence — one bad feed removes one commodity, it cannot poison the index
  • Timelock: every privileged action is propose → delay → execute, so holders get a reaction window
  • 239 automated tests, coverage-gated in CI; deploy scripts dry-run in emulation before any funds are spent

What you never have to worry about

  • A passed vote cannot move assets — governance changes bounded parameters only
  • We never hold client money and are never the custodian
  • Every rule is enforced on-chain, and the rules themselves are immutable

The index is a re-normalised weighted mean over active assets — published, with the full mathematics, in the whitepaper.
