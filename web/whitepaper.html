Planet — an on-chain commodity purchasing-power index.

This page summarises the Planet protocol. The full Chinese manuscript is maintained internally and is available on request to partners. Nothing here is investment advice.

1. Overview

Planet (PLT) aggregates the real prices of a basket of global commodities — energy, metals and agriculture — into an on-chain purchasing-power index (PPI), and expresses that index as a TEP-74 token on TON. A wallet can mint PLT by depositing value at the current PPI, and burn PLT to redeem against the basket.

Phase 1 status. Planet is a proof-of-concept synthetic index token. It holds no real commodity reserve and is not a fully-collateralised stablecoin. The full “1:1 commodity reserve” variant is a future phase that requires an RWA custodian and regulatory compliance.

3. The index (PPI)

The owner configures a weighted basket (e.g. WTI 40% · Gold 35% · Copper 25%) and locks base prices on-chain. A relayer submits signed per-asset prices; PlanetCore recomputes the PPI from the live basket. An asset flagged as frozen is excluded and the remaining weights are re-normalised.

4. Mint / burn and fees

Minting deposits value and issues PLT at the current PPI, net of a tiered fee (45 / 30 / 25 split across burn / reserve / staking). Burning goes through the TEP-74 jetton wallet and is mirrored back to core accounting so supply stays consistent.

5. Stability mechanisms

  • Band circuit-breaker: minting freezes when the PPI deviates >15% from peg.
  • Fee slope: fees rise above a 5% deviation; redemption is asymmetric.
  • Arbitrage incentive: a fee rebate when the index is rich, to pull it back.
  • Stabilisation bonds & risk fund: absorb selling pressure below peg; part of each fee funds a risk reserve.

6. Oracle

Prices are sourced off-chain from two independent feeds, combined as a median, and signed with ed25519. PlanetCore verifies against an M-of-N signer set (distinct signers enforced) and applies guards: strictly increasing timestamps, a single-update price-jump limit, and automatic freeze on source divergence.

7. Governance

All privileged actions require the owner. Ownership can be migrated to a multisig wallet and routed through a timelock: Propose → delay → Execute, giving holders a reaction window. Set the timelock’s owner to a multisig for M-of-N plus delay.

8. Status & roadmap

  • Now: TON testnet; contracts implemented and tested; multi-source oracle live.
  • Next: third-party audit; production oracle sources; key separation; multisig owner.
  • Later: 1:1 commodity reserve via an RWA custodian, proof-of-reserve and compliance.

9. Disclaimer

This is a technical proposal and a proof-of-concept, not investment advice. It is not audited. Commodity prices are volatile and an early-stage index token may diverge materially from its theoretical PPI. Do not use with value you cannot afford to lose.
