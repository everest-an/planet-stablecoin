Mint / redeem PLT

Calls PlanetCore directly from your own wallet — no backend, no custody, no permission. The preview below mirrors the contract's own integer arithmetic, so the number you see is the number the chain will compute.

Minting pulls real test USDC. mint(uint64 deposit) takes deposit USDC (6 decimals) from your wallet via transferFrom and mints PLT against it at the on-chain index. You will be asked to approve the amount first. This is Circle's Arbitrum Sepolia test USDC — worth nothing — and PLT is not a redemption right: redeeming pays test USDC back from the reserve, whatever is in it.
