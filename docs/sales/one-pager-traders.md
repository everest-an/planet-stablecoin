## The problem you already know

- **$2.5 trillion** of global trade finance demand goes unmet (ADB, 2025). In **Africa** the gap is **$74–92 billion** — about **5.4%** of the continent's merchandise trade.
- In **West Africa only ~25%** of trade is supported by trade finance — versus **60%+** in advanced economies. In **India**, export credit covers just **28.5%** of what shipment volumes require.
- When finance *is* available it is expensive: letters of credit run **2–5%**, and the working-capital loans that replace them average **~15% a year**.
- Meanwhile the infrastructure to represent, transfer and finance real commodity exposure on-chain now exists — **DMCC and VARA signed a framework for tokenised commodities in October 2025**, with the first instrument (a 1,971 kg silver bar) issued under it.

**You have the commodity, the licence and the clients. You do not have the technology — and building it takes 6–12 months.**
