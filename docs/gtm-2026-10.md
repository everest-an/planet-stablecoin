Written 2026-10-27, after the MVP shipped. Every claim here is checkable against the live site.

---

## 1. What is actually sellable today

| Asset | State | Money path |
|---|---|---|
| `/metals` — read every ounce you hold, one-click buy via Rango | **live** | none yet (no fee taken) |

**We are pre-revenue on Planet.** That is the first thing to fix, and it is a decision, not a build:
either take a fee on the swap, or do not call this a business yet.

## 2. The one-sentence pitch

> **See every tokenised ounce you hold, across wallets, in one place — and buy more in one click,
> from any chain.**

Not "commodity-backed stablecoin". Not "purchasing power index". The thing that works today is a
**portfolio and an on-ramp for on-chain metals**, and it works because it holds nothing and promises
nothing.

## 3. Three segments, in priority order

**A. Crypto holders who already own gold tokens** (fastest, smallest)
They hold PAXG or XAUT, spread across wallets and chains, and have no single view. The tool is
useful to them in one session with no wallet connection required to see the value proposition.

**B. People who want gold exposure but find the current experience hostile** (bigger, slower)
They know they should hold some gold, they have a wallet, and every existing route asks them to
pick a chain, a token, a bridge and a DEX. Our answer is one button.

**C. Protocols that need commodity prices** (slowest, largest contract value)
Perp DEXes, RWA lenders, structured products. They need a feed, not a UI. Six-month sales cycle.

**Start with A and B.** C is a separate motion with a separate deck and should not be mixed into
the consumer funnel.

**Reply to "how do I buy gold on-chain"**

> Rango routes it from wherever your USDC is.
> Caveat up front: only two tokens are credible today (PAXG, XAUT) and PAXG is unavailable in the EU.

## 6. First 30 / 60 / 90

**Day 0–30: prove the funnel exists**
- Take a fee on the swap (0.2–0.5%) or explicitly say "free while in beta".
- Instrument three events: page view, wallet connect, quote requested, swap executed.
- Target: 100 visits, 10 quotes, 1 executed swap from someone who is not us.
- Ship the "your total in ounces" share card — that is the viral unit.

**Day 31–60: find the one segment that repeats**
- Post in 5 communities, measure which one produces quotes (not clicks).
- Add the second gold token's chain if the data says people arrive from Base.
- Target: 3 swaps/week from strangers.

**Day 61–90: either raise the ceiling or stop**
- If swaps are repeating: add a fee, and start the audit + B2B feed conversations in parallel.
- If they are not: the answer is that the demand is not there at this size, and the honest move is
  to keep it as a free tool and pivot the effort to segment C.

## 8. Claims we must not make

- "Audited" — the only audit that exists is a self-review in an auditor's format
  (`docs/audit-simulated-2026-10.md`), and it says so in its first line.
- "Commodity-backed" — the reserve is a stablecoin; the token tracks a price.
- "Stable" — it is not a stablecoin and the site says so.
- "Works in the EU" for PAXG — it does not, under MiCA.

Every one of those has been removed from the site once already. Do not put them back in a tweet.
